Good questions

Keep the answers simple.

How many people fit under the 6×9m tent?

Erectus currently publishes 40 seated or about 50–60 cocktail-style as the core guide. Layout, dance floor and furniture change what feels comfortable.

Can I add sound and lighting?

In this upgraded concept, yes. The 3D scene lets you add an HB production brief, then HB confirms the system, availability, power, crew and final price.

Is the 3D plan an engineering drawing?

No. It is a sales and planning tool. Anchoring, rigging, power, laser safety and any structural requirements still need site-specific approval.

Does it work on phones?

The demo is designed for touch as well as mouse controls. The production version should prioritise one-finger placement, pinch zoom and large controls.
